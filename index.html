Appliances & pumps

Running watts × quantity gives each item's steady draw. Surge watts is the instantaneous peak while a motor starts — same as running watts for purely resistive loads (heaters, elements). Backup duty % is how much of the overnight backup window it actually runs — that's what sizes the battery, separately from the peak sizing above.
